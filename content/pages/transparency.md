## Why we publish this

A gift to a ministry you've never heard of takes trust. We'd rather earn that trust by telling you exactly where things stand — including the parts that aren't finished yet — than by staying quiet about them.

KOTHOM is early in its life as a formal organization. The filings below are the legal and financial groundwork that has to be in place before we can say things like "tax deductible" or display a registration number. Where a filing isn't done, we say so, instead of leaving the page vague or filling in a number that doesn't exist.

As each filing is completed, this page updates to show it — and nothing above changes about how the ministry operates. We've been doing the work of helping families since before any of these filings existed; the paperwork is what lets us do it at a larger scale, accept tax-deductible gifts, and be held to the same standard as any established nonprofit.
