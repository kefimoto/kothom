Every August, we hear the same worry from the parents we work with: the
school year is starting, and there isn't money left over for backpacks,
folders, pencils, or the long list of supplies a teacher sends home on the
first day. It's a small thing next to a utility bill or rent, and it's also
not small at all — no parent wants to send their child into a new classroom
without what the other kids have.

So each summer we put together a basic supply drive for the families
already connected to the ministry. It isn't complicated. We ask what grade
each child is entering, we ask what their teacher requested, and we do our
best to have it ready before the first bell rings.

## How it works

If you're a family we're already working with, you don't need to fill out
anything new — just let us know at your next check-in, or call the office,
and we'll add your children to the list. If you're new to KOTHOM and could
use help this year, reach out through our [Get Help](/get-help) page or by
phone, and we'll get you connected.

## How to help

If you'd like to contribute supplies or help cover the cost of them, the
easiest way to reach us is by [contacting the ministry directly](/contact).
We'll let you know what's still needed as the list comes together.

This is the same thing we try to do all year: notice what a family is
actually carrying, and see what we can lift.
