## Visual notes (informed DESIGN.md)

- Two alternating background colors only: near-black and a warm cream/off-white. No gradients or blends between them.
- Display headings use a bold, outlined/embossed gothic-serif capital style (approximate hue: silver/pewter fill on both backgrounds). Body copy uses a plain, legible serif.
- Named fonts per the client: **Cinzel Decorative** and **Augustea Open** (per client, 2026-07-20). Cinzel Decorative is free/Google Fonts (OFL); Augustea Open is a commercial font not licensed for this build — DESIGN.md substitutes Cinzel (Cinzel Decorative's non-decorative sibling) as a free lookalike until/unless a webfont license is purchased.
- One warm terracotta accent color for caption bars and filled buttons; a muted tan-gold for footer section labels/underlines.
- All corners are sharp (no rounding) throughout the deck.
- No shadows or glassmorphism anywhere — flat color blocks and full-bleed photography only.
